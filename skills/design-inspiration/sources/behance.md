# Behance

**Best for:** full brand identity case studies, motion branding, packaging and
editorial systems. It shows how a mark behaves across applications, not just
the mark.

**Access:** the browser. Search is public, no login needed. Plain fetches are
blocked (403).

## Searching

- URL: `https://www.behance.net/search/projects/<query>`
- Filter chips at the top (graphic design, branding, motion graphics, logo
  animation) narrow the results.
- Strong queries: `motion identity`, `brand identity system`, `visual identity
  motion`, `logo animation`, plus a sector or style word.

## Extracting

Screenshot the grid to judge it, then collect the project card links and cover
images with `javascript_exec`. Project pages hold the studio name, the full case
study and often embedded video. Open the strongest projects to get better
frames and the credit.

## Credits

Project pages name the studio or designer. Credit them and link the project.

## Limits

- Covers are often composites. Open the project to see the real work.
- There are sponsored or "Promoted" cards. Skip them.
