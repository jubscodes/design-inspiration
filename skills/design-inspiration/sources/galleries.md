# Curated galleries: Godly, Land-book, Lapa Ninja, Mobbin

**Access:** the browser only. Plain fetches return 403 or bot-check pages, and
you must never get past those yourself.

| Site | Best for | URL | Login |
|---|---|---|---|
| Godly | Polished, motion-heavy websites | `godly.website` | No |
| Land-book | Landing pages by category | `land-book.com` | No |
| Lapa Ninja | Landing pages; strong fintech and SaaS coverage | `lapa.ninja` | No |
| Mobbin | Real production app screens and flows (iOS, Android, web) | `mobbin.com` | Yes |

## How to use

Navigate, take a screenshot to judge the grid, then extract the card links and
images with `javascript_exec` (see [README.md](README.md) for the output limits).
For Mobbin, ask the user to sign in themselves in your tab.

For products with both a web and an app surface, pair Mobbin (app flows) with
Godly or Awwwards (web).
